Introduction:

The USJ-R E-Clinic is a mobile-based digital clinic management application designed to improve the way
medical and dental services are managed within the University of San Jose–Recoletos (USJ-R). The
application aims to reduce dependence on paper-based patient records by providing a centralized and
organized platform for managing patient profiles, clinic appointments, consultations, medical and dental
records, prescriptions, and related clinic services.

In a university environment,the clinic handles students and university personnel who may requiremedical
consultations, nursing assistance, dental services, or other clinic support. Managing these records
manually can result in delays, misplaced documents, repetitive data entry, and difficulty retrieving
previous patient information. The USJ-R E-Clinic addresses these challenges by providing authorized users
with a digital system for efficiently accessing and updating information according to their assigned
responsibilities.

The application supports four patient types: Students, Faculty, Administrative Personnel, and NonTeaching Personnel. 
It also supports R.E.A.D.S./Working Scholars and assigned Medical Staff, consisting of
Nurses, Doctors, and Dentists. Each role has a separate dashboard and a defined set of functions and
access permissions. The system is intended to provide a modern, organized, user-friendly, and secure
approach to clinic operations while maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of patient health
information.


Project Overview:

The E-Clinic modernizes healthcare management for the USJ-R community through a mobile application.
Patients can manage their own profiles, appointments, permitted health records, prescriptions, and
notifications. R.E.A.D.S./Working Scholars perform administrative and registration tasks. Medical Staff
can search and retrieve patient records and perform clinical functions appropriate to their professional
role.

The application will be developed using .NET MAUI and C#, targeting Android devices and tested using
an Android Emulator. Data will be stored using the project's selected database approach. The system
will use role-based authentication and authorization so that the dashboard and available functions
depend on the authenticated user's role.

Main objective: create a secure, organized, and accessible clinic management application that improves
clinic operations and provides an appropriate user experience for students, faculty, administrative
personnel, non-teaching personnel, R.E.A.D.S./Working Scholars, nurses, doctors, and dentists.

● In scope: role-based authentication and dashboards; role-specific public registration; patient
profile management; appointment booking and management; medical and dental records;
prescriptions (Clinic-Given/Take-Home); medicine inventory and dispensing; APE lab results
(Faculty/Administrative/Non-Teaching only); patient search and check-in; notifications and
announcements

● Out ofscope: public self-registration for Medical Staff (Nurse, Doctor, Dentist) accounts — these
are clinic-assigned only
● Target platforms: Android, developed using .NET MAUI and C#, tested using an Android
Emulator
